Good research software often feels quiet. It does not ask the user to perform
attention; it preserves attention. The interface lets a person compare adjacent
claims, trace a source, postpone a conclusion, and revise without losing the
path that led there.

This is one reason I am drawn to human-centered AI as a design frame
[@shneiderman2020human]. Reliability is not only an engineering property of a
model. It is also a property of the human system around the model: the logs,
views, warnings, defaults, and rituals through which people decide what to trust.

The open question is how small such tools can be while still changing behavior.
Sometimes the most valuable feature is not automation, but a page that refuses
to hide disagreement too quickly.
